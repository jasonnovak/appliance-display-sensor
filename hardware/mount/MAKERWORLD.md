Title: Appliance Display Sensor - Pi Camera Mount

A removable camera mount for reading an appliance's seven-segment countdown with
a Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W and Camera Module 3 Standard. Includes the magnetic base,
Pi lid, camera cover, cable cover and arm. The open-source sensor publishes to Home
Assistant over MQTT; an optional self-hosted Google bridge adds spoken countdown
answers on the speaker asked.

Files include the final H2C 3MF, its five matching STLs, corresponding STEP exports
and five build photos. The set uses the recessed-head base, Pi lid, thicker camera
front cover, closed-end cable cover and overlap-nose arm. See
`print-project/README.md` for saved settings and mesh verification.
Requires six 10 x 3 mm magnets, M3x8 socket-head screw/M3 nut and a 300 mm
Standard-to-Mini camera cable. Check the README's magnet-insertion pause before
printing. No G-code or validated universal printer profile is supplied.

The listing cover shows the mount installed on the dryer. Supporting photos show
the assembled mount, electronics, separate printed parts and camera end.

Software, setup instructions and updates:
https://github.com/jasonnovak/appliance-display-sensor

The owner reports physical testing complete on the reference installation and
published the MakerWorld listing on 2026-11-11. Other printer/material combinations
remain unverified.
